<h2>About this module</h2>
    <p>You will take a side on a low-stakes question. An AI will help you argue it. Then you will read the other side's case. You will rate your certainty three times.</p>
    <p>By the end, you will be able to:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>Define LLM sycophancy and explain why AI training rewards it.</li>
      <li>Describe how AI agreement raises certainty and hinders navigating disagreements.</li>
      <li>Detect sycophancy by recording when the AI agrees or pushes back.</li>
      <li>Reach a shared position with someone who disagrees.</li>
      <li>Write a prompt that asks AI to challenge your view.</li>
    </ol>
