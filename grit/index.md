<h2>About this module</h2>
    <p>You will solve two pairs of problems. Each pair shares one idea.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><span class="muted">Round 1, AI first.</span> AI solves the first problem. You solve the second alone.</li>
      <li><span class="muted">Round 2, struggle first.</span> You work alone on the first problem, with hints. Then you solve the second alone.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>You need an AI tool, paper and a pen.</p>
    <p>By the end, you will be able to:</p>
    <ol>
      <li>Distinguish performance with AI from lasting learning.</li>
      <li>Explain why productive struggle improves learning.</li>
      <li>Describe how relying on AI for hard problems compounds over time.</li>
      <li>Identify which steps of a problem you cannot do without AI.</li>
      <li>Set a personal rule for when to struggle before asking AI.</li>
    </ol>
