<h1>Introduction</h1>

<h2>The MAHA report</h2>
<p>In May 2025, the US government published the MAHA health report. It cited studies that do not exist. It also stretched the findings of real studies. This was likely done with AI help (Tuquero 2025).</p>
<h2>Three types of hallucination</h2>
<p>A hallucination is fluent, confident LLM output. It is false, unsupported, or unfaithful to its sources.</p>
<div class="table-scroll">
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Type</th><th>Definition</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Factual hallucination</td><td>A false or unsupported factual claim</td><td>Wrong date, statistic, or rule</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Source hallucination</td><td>A fabricated or misattributed citation</td><td>A paper that does not exist</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Source misrepresentation</td><td>A real source whose content is misrepresented</td><td>A real study cited for a finding it does not support</td></tr>
