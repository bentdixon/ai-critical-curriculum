AI Critical Curriculum Self-paced demos

Using AI critically

Each module is a short, self-paced activity for one learner, adapted from a 45-minute classroom lesson. Most take 30 to 40 minutes.

Some steps ask you to use an AI tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Open it in another tab. Your answers stay in this browser and are not sent anywhere. At the end of each module you can print or download your work.

  • Identifying AI hallucinations

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool

    Argue with an AI until it agrees with something false, then check the sources it gives you.

  • AI sycophancy and working with others

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool

    Track how your certainty changes after an agreeable AI, and after hearing the other side from a person.

  • Grit: struggling with hard problems

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool, paper and pen

    Solve two pairs of problems, once with AI first and once on your own first, and see what you can still do alone.
