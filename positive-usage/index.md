<h2>About this module</h2>
<p>Uncritical AI use may reduce learning. But intentional usage can be effective.</p>
<p>You will learn Bloom’s levels of learning. You will use AI at each level. Your usage of AI must not offload your reasoning.</p>
<p>By the end, you will be able to:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Define levels of cognitive engagement.</li>
  <li>Explain the difference between cognitive offloading and cognitive support.</li>
  <li>Produce prompts that support critical engagement.</li>
</ol>
