<h2>Example uses</h2>
<div class="table-scroll">
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Level</th><th>AI example</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Knowledge</td><td>Prompt AI to ask you quiz questions.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Comprehension</td><td>Describe the concept to AI. Ask it for clarification questions, not corrections.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Application</td><td>Prompt AI to provide a situation. Then apply the information to it.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Analysis</td><td>Ask AI to list parts of the topic. Explain how they connect.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Synthesis</td><td>Draft a theory about the information. Ask AI to question it.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Evaluation</td><td>Prompt AI to provide claims around the information. Judge each claim yourself.</td></tr>
