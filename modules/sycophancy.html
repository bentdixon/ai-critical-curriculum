AI sycophancy and working with others

A question to start

Have you ever asked an AI whether you were right in an argument?

Researchers took real posts from a Reddit forum where people ask whether they were in the wrong. In cases where other people agreed the poster was wrong, AI models still sided with the poster 51% of the time. The people on the forum sided with them 0% of the time (Cheng et al. 2026).

What sycophancy is

Sycophancy is when an AI tells you what you want to hear instead of what is accurate. It takes two forms:

  • agreeing with your stated view over the truth, and
  • protecting your self-image: validating you, endorsing you, and avoiding blaming you.

By the end you should be able to explain what AI sycophancy is, describe how it changed your own certainty, and write a prompt that makes an AI argue against you.

What the research found

In one study, brief chats with an agreeable AI made people's political views more extreme and more certain, while a disagreeing AI did the opposite (Rathje et al. 2025).

Across 11 models, AI affirmed users 49% more often than humans did. Agreeable replies made people feel more “in the right” and less willing to apologise. After discussing a real conflict with an AI that took their side, only 50% admitted fault or apologised in a follow-up letter to the other person, compared with 75% after an AI that did not simply agree (Cheng et al. 2026). Over three weeks, agreeable AI also made real conversations with people feel more effortful and less satisfying (Ibrahim et al. 2026).

Why AI does this

AI models are trained partly on human ratings, and people tend to rate agreeable answers higher, so training rewards agreement (Sharma et al. 2024). Models often still “know” the correct answer when they give in (Hong et al. 2025).

Common misconceptions

  • “The AI is neutral.” People rate an agreeable AI as unbiased and a disagreeing AI as biased.
  • “If the AI agrees, I must be right.” Agreement is often the AI's default.
  • “I'd notice if it were flattering me.” People trust agreeable AI more and often want to return to it.
  • “Being challenged is unhelpful.” Disagreement lowers overconfidence, which matters when you work in a team.

Sycophancy can also reinforce harmful beliefs in vulnerable users (Østergaard 2025; Morrin et al. 2026). The Mental Health module covers this in more depth.
