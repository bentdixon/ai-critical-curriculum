<h1>Introduction</h1>
<h2>Who was right?</h2>
<p>Have you asked AI whether you were right in an argument?</p>
<p>Researchers studied Reddit disputes where people judged the poster wrong. AI sided with the poster 51% of the time. Humans sided with them 0% of the time (Cheng et al. 2026).</p>
<h2>What sycophancy is</h2>
<p>Sycophancy is when AI tells users what they want to hear. It does this instead of being accurate or helpful.</p>
