  <h2>Impacts on human relationships</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>AI is always available and designed to seem human. This can dull real-world empathy and emotion recognition (Head 2025).</li>
    <li>Users can form attachments to AI. These can trigger withdrawal when the AI is unavailable (Head 2025).</li>
    <li>Dependent users may trust AI more than human sources of support. This makes them vulnerable to hallucinated content (Head 2025).</li>
    <li>Heavy AI use may weaken the ability to judge AI replies. Dependency then increases reliance on false or reinforcing information (Head 2025).</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>The value of human connection</h2>
  <p>Human connection is a strong protective factor for mental health (Holt-Lunstad 2024). Research links school difficulties with mental disorders and substance abuse (Kalam et al. 2024).</p>
  <p>Human help for study exists too. Look for tutors, study groups and office hours at your institution.</p>
