<h1>Write your essay</h1>
<div class="prompt"><p class="text">Reflect on your own AI use. Describe how you use AI today. Explain what works and what worries you. Then describe how you want to use AI from now on. Name at least one specific change you will make.</p></div>
<ul>
  <li>Aim for 600–800 words.</li>
  <li>Write in the first person, in plain prose.</li>
  <li>Write the essay yourself, without AI.</li>
  <li>Use your answers from the last two pages.</li>
</ul>
