Grit: struggling with hard problems when AI is available

Is getting the answer learning?

Think of the last hard problem you used AI for. Could you redo it alone?

About this module

You will solve two pairs of problems. Each pair shares one idea.

  • Round 1, AI first. AI solves the first problem. You solve the second alone.
  • Round 2, struggle first. You work alone on the first problem, with hints. Then you solve the second alone.

You need an AI tool, paper and a pen.

By the end, you will be able to:

  1. Distinguish performance with AI from lasting learning.
  2. Explain why productive struggle improves learning.
  3. Describe how relying on AI for hard problems compounds over time.
  4. Identify which steps of a problem you cannot do without AI.
  5. Set a personal rule for when to struggle before asking AI.

    The research

    Nearly 1,000 students practised maths with plain GPT-4. It raised their practice grades by 48%. Without it, they scored 17% below peers (Bastani et al. 2025).

    Your two rounds are a demonstration, not an experiment. The problems differ, and the order was fixed. Treat your result as something to think about.

    Key concepts

    ConceptDefinition
    Desirable difficultyHarder practice feels slower but is retained better (Bjork 1994)
    Productive failureStruggling before instruction beats instruction first (Sinha and Kapur 2021)
    Skill decayUnused skills fade, and thinking skills fade fastest (Arthur et al. 1998)

    Why the cap compounds

    Confidence grows from doing hard things yourself (Bandura 1977). Less self-confident people hand more thinking to AI (Lee et al. 2025). Each hard problem given to AI skips useful practice. This makes the next problem feel harder.

    Common misconceptions

    • “If I got the right answer with AI, I learned it.” Performance with a tool is not learning.
    • “Struggling means I’m doing it wrong.” Struggle is often a sign that learning is happening.
    • “AI always harms learning.” Well-designed tutors can help (Kestin et al. 2025).

    Set your struggle rule

    Write a personal rule for when to struggle before asking AI. For example: “15 minutes alone, then hints only.”
