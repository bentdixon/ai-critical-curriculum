<h1>Why struggle</h1>
<h2>Key concepts</h2>

<h2>Why the cap compounds</h2>
<p>Confidence grows from doing hard things yourself (Bandura 1977). Less self-confident people hand more thinking to AI (Lee et al. 2025). Each hard problem given to AI skips useful practice. This makes the next problem feel harder.</p>
<h2>Common misconceptions</h2>
<ul>
  <li>“If I got the right answer with AI, I learned it.” Performance with a tool is not learning.</li>
  <li>“Struggling means I’m doing it wrong.” Struggle is often a sign that learning is happening.</li>
  <li>“AI always harms learning.” Well-designed tutors can help (Kestin et al. 2025).</li>
</ul>
