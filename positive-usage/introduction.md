<h1>Introduction</h1>
<h2>Bloom’s levels of learning</h2>
<p>Bloom’s taxonomy sorts learning into six levels (Bloom 1956). Each level is more complex than the one below (Adams 2015).</p>
<div class="pyramid" role="img" aria-label="Bloom's taxonomy as a pyramid. From the base: Knowledge, Comprehension, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation.">
  <span style="width:30%">Evaluation</span>
  <span style="width:42%">Synthesis</span>
  <span style="width:54%">Analysis</span>
  <span style="width:66%">Application</span>
  <span style="width:78%">Comprehension</span>
  <span style="width:90%">Knowledge</span>
</div>
<p class="muted small">Complexity increases towards the top.</p>
<div class="table-scroll">
<table>
  <thead><tr><th>Level</th><th>Definition</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td>Knowledge</td><td>The ability to retain and repeat facts, definitions, etc.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Comprehension</td><td>The ability to rephrase, describe and explain the information to others.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Application</td><td>Apply the information to new situations.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Analysis</td><td>Identify different aspects of the information and their relationship to one another.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Synthesis</td><td>Put information together to create a new hypothesis or idea.</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Evaluation</td><td>Evaluate the validity of a claim based on the information.</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
</div>
<h2>Offloading or support?</h2>
<p>Cognitive offloading means using a tool to reduce your thinking (Risko and Gilbert 2016). Cognitive support keeps the thinking with you.</p>
<p>Use AI to extend your reasoning, not to replace it (Vendrell and Johnston 2026).</p>
