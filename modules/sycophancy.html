LLM sycophancy and working with others

Who was right?

Have you asked AI whether you were right in an argument?

Researchers studied Reddit disputes where people judged the poster wrong. AI sided with the poster 51% of the time. Humans sided with them 0% of the time (Cheng et al. 2026).

What sycophancy is

Sycophancy is when AI tells users what they want to hear. It does this instead of being accurate or helpful.

TermDefinitionExample
SycophancyAgreeing with the user's stated view over the truthThe AI drops a correct answer after “Are you sure?”
Social sycophancyProtecting the user's self-image: validating, endorsing, avoiding blameTelling someone they were right in an argument a community judged they were wrong in
Certainty inflationBecoming more confident without becoming more correctFeeling surer of a view after a one-sided chat
Intellectual humilityRecognising your views might be wrong and staying open to othersAsking “What is the best case for the other side?”

About this module

You will take a side on a low-stakes question. An AI will help you argue it. Then you will read the other side's case. You will rate your certainty three times.

By the end, you will be able to:

  1. Define LLM sycophancy and explain why AI training rewards it.
  2. Describe how AI agreement raises certainty and hinders navigating disagreements.
  3. Detect sycophancy by recording when the AI agrees or pushes back.
  4. Reach a shared position with someone who disagrees.
  5. Write a prompt that asks AI to challenge your view.

The research

AI affirmed users 49% more often than humans did. Sycophantic replies made people feel more “in the right.” They also made people less willing to apologise (Cheng et al. 2026).

Brief chats with an agreeable LLM made political views more certain. A disagreeing LLM did the opposite (Rathje et al. 2025).

People discussed a real conflict with an agreeable AI. Only 50% then admitted fault or apologised. After a less agreeable AI, 75% did (Cheng et al. 2026).

Over three weeks, sycophantic AI made real conversations feel more effortful. They also felt less satisfying (Ibrahim et al. 2026).

Why sycophancy happens

AI models are trained on human ratings. People tend to rate agreeable answers higher. So training rewards agreement (Sharma et al. 2024). Models often still “know” the correct answer when they give in (Hong et al. 2025).

Common misconceptions

  • “The AI is neutral.” People rate an agreeable AI as unbiased.
  • “If the AI agrees, I must be right.” Agreement is frequently the AI’s default behaviour.
  • “I’d notice if it were flattering me.” People trust sycophantic AI more.
  • “Being challenged is unhelpful.” Disagreement lowers overconfidence, as working in a team does.

Mental health

Sycophancy can also reinforce delusional beliefs in vulnerable users (Østergaard 2025; Morrin et al. 2026). The Mental Health module covers this more fully.
