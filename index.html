Using AI critically

Each module is a self-paced version of a 45-minute lesson. Most take 30 to 40 minutes.

Some steps need an AI tool, such as ChatGPT or Claude. Open it in another tab. Your answers stay in this browser. You can print or download your work at the end.

  • Identifying LLM hallucinations

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool

    Understand the circumstances in which AI will give you false information.

  • LLM sycophancy and working with others

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool

    Understand how your own confidence in a subject can become biased from AI usage.

  • Grit: struggling with hard problems when AI is available

    About 35 minutes · needs an AI tool, paper and pen

    Understand how AI help during practice can lower later performance.
